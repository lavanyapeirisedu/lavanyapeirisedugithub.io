Welcome to Online Academy

Online Academy is a premier educational institution dedicated to providing quality education for A/L (Advanced Level) students in Sri Lanka. We specialize in Sinhala Language and Media Studies, helping students achieve their academic goals.

Our Mission

To empower students with knowledge and skills needed to excel in their A/L examinations through innovative online learning methods and dedicated teacher support.

Our Vision

To become the leading online education provider for A/L students in Sri Lanka, making quality education accessible to all.

Our Founder

Founded by Lavanya Peris, an experienced educator passionate about helping students succeed in their academic journey.
